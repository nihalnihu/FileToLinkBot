About MRDb Cinema Stream

MRDb (Movie Research Database) brings movie discovery, trailers, verified soundtracks, and fast Telegram-powered HD video streaming together in one clean experience.

⚡
Adaptive Web Streaming
Automatically converts MKV / HEVC / surround files to browser-ready H.264 MP4 with 720p, 480p, 360p, and Original playback.
⏱
Instant Full-Timeline Seek
Displays total movie duration upfront, supports timeline scrubbing, double-tap ±10s skip, playback speed, and Picture-in-Picture.
📱
Native MRDb App Player
Switch to MRDb on Android for hardware-accelerated MKV playback, multi-audio tracks, embedded subtitles, and watch-progress sync.
🤖
Telegram Bot Companion
Forward or upload any video file to @FileToSpeedLink_Bot to generate direct stream links and instant web theater pages.
📲
Get MRDb for Android
Open directly if installed or grab the latest APK release from GitHub.
Notice

This service generates temporary stream endpoints from files shared through Telegram bots. Please respect content copyrights and local guidelines while streaming.
